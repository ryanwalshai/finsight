FinSight — personal finance dashboard for iPhone

FinSight has no account and no server, so there is nothing to sign into, nothing to reset and nothing held anywhere for anyone to look up. Almost everything below you can settle on the phone in your hand.

Getting started

Open the app and take the second step of setup: Load demo data fills every screen with fifteen months of realistic figures so you can see what it does before typing anything of your own. Loading it again later, from Settings → Backup & data, replaces whatever is there — so export a backup first if you have started for real.

Starting with your own figures takes about ten minutes the first time: add your accounts and what each says today. After that it is a couple of minutes at the end of each month.

The monthly round

  • Update balances — type what each account says today.
  • Add spending — what the month cost, and what you put by.
  • Add payslip — drop the PDF in and it reads gross, tax, NI and pension off it for you to check.
  • Check your goals — the part that makes the rest worth doing.

Long-pressing the app icon on the Home Screen jumps straight to the first three.

Common questions

What does a figure on a card mean?

Every card has an i in its corner. It says what that card is counting, which months it used and what it left out. Nothing in FinSight is a number without a stated source.

Can I connect my bank?

No, deliberately. FinSight has no network access at all — it never contacts a bank, a broker or any server, and it cannot move money. Everything in it is what you typed or what it read off a payslip you gave it.

Where is my data, and is it backed up?

It is inside the app on your iPhone. It is included in an iCloud or encrypted local device backup the way any other app's data is, and it goes when the app goes. The reliable copy is the one you make: Settings → Backup & data → Export backup hands a JSON file to the share sheet, so it can go to Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop. Restore from Files brings it back, on this phone or a new one.

I forgot my PIN.

Nobody can reset it — there is no account behind it and no key held anywhere else. If you have a backup file, delete the app, reinstall it and restore. If you do not, the data is not recoverable. This is the trade for an app that keeps nothing on a server.

Face ID is not working.

The app lock lives at Settings → General → Lock with Face ID. iOS does the checking; if it refuses too many times, iOS falls back to your device passcode, which always works. If Face ID is off for FinSight in the iPhone's own Settings → Face ID & Passcode → Other Apps, the card in FinSight cannot turn it on.

I lost data, or it looks wrong.

Check Settings → Backup & data → Storage first — it says how much data the app is holding. If a figure is wrong rather than missing, the card's i usually explains it: most surprises are a month with no balance recorded, which the app leaves out of averages rather than counting as zero.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. The whole app is in the download; there is nothing to fetch. It behaves the same in airplane mode as anywhere else.

Reporting a problem

Bugs, questions and requests go to the public issue tracker: github.com/hvrkwd24ct-prog/finsight/issues. There is no email address on purpose — an inbox for an app that collects nothing would be the only place your details ever ended up, and an answer in the open helps the next person with the same question.

Useful in a report: the app version from Settings → General → Help and support, your iOS version, and what you did just before it went wrong. Please leave your own figures out of it — the tracker is public, and nothing about diagnosing a problem requires knowing your balances.

Not financial advice

FinSight keeps your own figures and does arithmetic on them. The strategies it shows are well-known rules of thumb written for a general audience, and the projections assume the future resembles the recent past. None of it is regulated financial advice. If a decision matters, take it to an adviser who is regulated to give advice on it.

Privacy

The full policy is at privacy.html, and the same words are readable inside the app at Settings → Backup & data → Privacy — including on a plane. The short version: FinSight collects nothing.

FinSight is a personal project. It is not affiliated with any bank, broker or financial institution, and none of the providers named inside the app are affiliated with it.
