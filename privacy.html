Privacy Policy

FinSight — personal finance dashboard for iPhone

Last updated 26 August 2026

FinSight collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything you type stays in the app's own storage on your device, and the app has no way to send it anywhere.

What is collected

None of the following are collected, transmitted, sold, shared or seen by anyone but you: your name, your balances, your accounts, your income, your spending, your goals, your payslips, your device identifiers, your location, your contacts, or your usage of the app.

Because nothing leaves the device, there is nothing to request a copy of, correct or have deleted by us — deleting the app, or using Settings → Backup & data → Reset all data, removes everything.

Where your figures live

Everything you enter is stored locally in the app's own container on your iPhone. It is covered by your device passcode and iOS data protection, and by your device backup if you have one — an iCloud or encrypted local device backup will include FinSight's data, in the same way it includes any other app's, and that backup is governed by Apple's terms rather than ours.

Payslips and backups

  • Payslip PDFs are read on your device, by code that ships inside the app. The file is never uploaded. Only the figures you confirm are kept.
  • Backup files are created only when you ask for one. They are handed to the iOS share sheet, and where they go from there — Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email — is entirely your choice. A backup deliberately excludes your app-lock PIN.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you turn on the app lock, FinSight asks iOS to verify you. The check happens inside the system: your face or fingerprint is never made available to the app, is never stored by the app, and never leaves your device. FinSight is told only whether the check passed.

The network

FinSight makes no network requests of any kind. This is enforced rather than merely intended: the app's page sets a Content-Security-Policy of connect-src 'none', so the browser engine refuses an outbound request, and the app is built without the network entitlement. The app works identically in airplane mode.

Tracking, advertising and third parties

There is no tracking, no advertising identifier, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no advertising, and no third-party SDK of any kind. No data is shared with data brokers or anyone else, because no data is collected.

Children

FinSight is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Not financial advice

FinSight keeps your own figures and does arithmetic on them. The strategies it shows are well-known rules of thumb written for a general audience, and the projections assume the future resembles the recent past. None of it is regulated financial advice. If a decision matters, take it to an adviser who is regulated to give advice on it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. If FinSight ever started collecting anything, that would be said here plainly and before the fact.

Questions about this policy

There is no address to write to, and that is deliberate: an inbox for an app that collects nothing would be the only place any of your details ever ended up. Questions and problems go to the public issue tracker, where the answer is visible to everybody who had the same question. The support page answers the common ones already.

FinSight is a personal project. It is not affiliated with any bank, broker or financial institution, and none of the providers named inside the app are affiliated with it.
